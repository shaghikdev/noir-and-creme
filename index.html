Our Story

More Than Coffee.
A Moment to Savour.

NOIR & CRÈME was born from a simple belief: that the most beautiful moments of the day deserve a beautiful setting. Inspired by the timeless cafés of Paris — their marble counters, their brass lamps, their unhurried mornings — we crafted a space where every cup and every croissant feels considered.

Our beans are carefully sourced and lightly roasted. Our pastries are made fresh each morning by hand. And our tables are set with the same quiet attention you'd find on a quiet street in the Marais.

This is not fast coffee. This is coffee as it was meant to be — slow, warm, and savoured.

— The House of NOIR & CRÈME

— The Coffee

Our Signature Noir Espresso.

A single-origin blend, roasted to reveal notes of dark chocolate, toasted almond, and a whisper of caramel. Served in hand-thrown porcelain, it is the quiet heart of our café.

Every cup is pulled by hand, at the precise moment the crema begins to bloom.

— The Pastry

The Morning Croissant.

Laminated over three days with French butter, our croissants are baked before dawn each morning. The result: a shatteringly crisp exterior, a honeycombed interior, and the unmistakable aroma of a Parisian boulangerie.

Served warm, with house-made confiture.

ButterFrench AOP
Proofed72 Hours
BakedBefore Dawn

Pâtisserie of the Day.

Each afternoon, our pâtissier presents a small selection of seasonal desserts — a delicate tarte tatin, a glossy Paris-Brest, a vanilla bean mille-feuille. Each one is a miniature work of art, made to be shared — or not.
